# College Finder
College Finder is a website made by me (a sixth grader) for a project.
Steps to use:
1. Enter in your name, gender, and whether you go to private or public school
2. Specify what type of college you want to go in (optional)
3. Enter in your grades (mandatory) and enter your GPA (optional)
4. Click find college
5. It will tell you the most prestigious college that you will most likely get accepted into
